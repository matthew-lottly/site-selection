# Speaker Notes (Concise)

## Slide 1
Lead with the recommendation immediately. Mention this is a citywide result, not a single-neighborhood pick.

## Slide 2
Set the confidence frame: benchmark-gated, validated, and transparent about trade-offs.

## Slide 3
Clarify the business ask: choose a site with defensible demand and manageable risk.

## Slide 4
Emphasize reproducibility and anti-hallucination checks; this builds executive trust quickly.

## Slide 5
Walk from macro to micro. Stress that all finalists are real parcels, not hypothetical points.

## Slide 6
Keep the ranking simple. Do not read every row; focus on separation and alternatives.

## Slide 7
Explain why this site wins operationally and financially.

## Slide 8
Pause here. This is the core decision fork and likely where VP questions start.

## Slide 9
State stability accurately: strong but not universal. Credibility improves when limits are explicit.

## Slide 10
Demo the map in under 90 seconds: toggle FEMA, open dashboard, show scorecard and cannibalization tabs.

## Slide 11
Convert limitations into action items; show they are already planned, not overlooked.

## Slide 12
Close with a clear ask, timeline, and ownership. End on decision readiness.

## Q&A backup points

- Why no revenue forecast?
  No public store-level sales data exists to calibrate a defensible dollar model.

- Why include #2 alternate?
  It reduces cannibalization risk and preserves decision flexibility during diligence.

- Why trust the traffic metric?
  Counts were verified and filtered to avoid freeway-mainline misclassification.
